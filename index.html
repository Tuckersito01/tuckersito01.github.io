How it works

Three moves from plain to finished.

Load it on a melody

Insert VELTARA on any instrument or audio track: keys, synths, guitars, arps, vocal chops.

Pick a sound

Browse 120 presets by genre or press One Click Magic. It listens to your part and chooses a direction that fits it.

Shape it by hand

Drag the XY pad from warm to airy and from pure to textured, then dial in six macros: Texture, Movement, Space, Character, Depth and Density.

Seven modules. Each one has a personality.

Built for melodies

Heavy texture. The hook stays on top.

Melody Preservation

Pulls the texture back while a note speaks and carves a little space around 2 kHz, so the melody keeps cutting through.

Texture Follower

Reads your dynamics: play harder and the grains get denser and shorter; let a note ring and the space opens up.

Texture Evolution

Slow random motion reshapes the filter, grain position and density over 4 to 12 seconds, so a loop never sounds frozen.

Preset Morphing

Store two sounds as A and B and slide between them. Frequencies move on a musical scale and the position is saved with your session.

FAQ
Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the license is yours. Updates within version 1 are free.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to activate your license the first time. After that VELTARA runs offline, and audio processing never depends on a connection.

Will the glitch follow my project tempo?

Yes. Slices lock to your DAW's tempo and bar position, from half notes down to 1/32 and triplets. Press play in your DAW and the edits land on the grid.

Can I use it on vocals and drums?

Yes. It was designed around melodic parts, but stutters and tape stops on drum loops and vocal chops work well.

Can I release music made with the presets?

Yes. Everything you make with VELTARA is yours to release, royalty-free.

How many computers can I use?

Up to three at a time, for example a studio Mac, a laptop and a Windows PC. You can move an activation from your account.

How it works

Three moves from plain to finished.

Load it on a melody

Insert VELTARA on any instrument or audio track: keys, synths, guitars, arps, vocal chops.

Pick a sound

Browse 150 presets by genre in a visual browser. Each card shows how much texture, movement and space it brings.

Play it by hand

Four macros shape everything: Texture, Movement, Space, Character. Then send the XY pad to one effect and play it like an instrument.

The engine

Ten effects. Ten different algorithms.

Every mode is its own processing, not the same effect under another name. A tape delay wobbles and darkens; a digital one stays exact. A folder sounds metallic; a tube adds warmth.

GLITCH

Cuts your part into tempo-synced slices and edits them: retriggers, backspins, tape stops, reorders, thrown across the stereo field.

Chaos · Stutter · Reverse · Tape Stop · Shuffle
GRAIN

Live granular clouds from your own audio: size, density, position, pitch, reverse grains, harmonic intervals and Freeze.

64 grains · ±24 st · Freeze
FILTER

A resonant multimode filter that morphs between shapes and sweeps musically across the spectrum.

Cutoff · Resonance · Morph
DRIVE

Four saturation curves with anti-aliasing: soft dark tape, warm tube with even harmonics, hard fuzz and a metallic wave folder.

Tape · Tube · Fuzz · Fold
MOD FX

From a lush ensemble to a resonant jet flanger and a deep six-stage phaser.

Chorus · Ensemble · Flanger · Phaser
LO-FI

Worn-tape wobble that drifts on each side, bit and sample-rate reduction, and hiss that only plays while your part does.

Wow · Bits · Rate · Noise
DELAY

Echoes that duck under your playing. Digital is exact, Tape wobbles and saturates every pass, Diffuse smears, Reverse swells backwards.

Digital · Tape · Diffuse · Reverse
REVERB

A modulated eight-line reverb with octave shimmer, a low cut that keeps the bass clean, and a Freeze that holds the tail.

Size · Decay · Shimmer · Freeze
SPECTRAL

Works on the frequencies themselves: blurs them, shifts them and scatters partials into a glassy cloud.

Blur · Shift · Scatter
HARMONY

Stacks pitched voices around your melody, spread and detuned into choirs and fifths.

Voices · Spread · Detune
XY pad

A pad you play, not a pad you look at.

  • Pick the effect it plays. Each one has its own two axes: cutoff and resonance for the filter, feedback and width for the delay, size and density for grains.
  • The centre is your preset. Move away to explore, double-click to come back.
  • Smooth and click-free, audible while you drag, and saved with your session.
Factory library

150 presets. Every one sounds different.

Each preset is level-matched against the dry signal, so switching never jumps in volume, and checked against the rest of the library so no two sound alike.

FAQ
Is this a subscription?

Your choice. The Perpetual license is a one-time payment and it is yours. VELTARA Plus is a subscription that adds every monthly expansion pack.

How does rent-to-own work?

After 12 monthly payments of VELTARA Plus (or one yearly payment), your license turns into a perpetual one automatically: VELTARA is yours for good, on every computer, with nothing to do. If you cancel later, you keep VELTARA and the packs.

What is in an expansion pack?

New presets and textures, one pack a month, installed from the PACKS button inside the plugin. Some packs are free for everyone; Plus members get all of them.

Does it need an internet connection?

To activate (you approve the computer in your browser) and for a quick license check now and then: Perpetual works up to 60 days offline; Plus checks in around each renewal. No internet on the studio computer? Activate it offline with a file. Audio processing never depends on a connection.

Will the glitch follow my project tempo?

Yes. Slices lock to your DAW's tempo and bar position, from half notes down to 1/32 and triplets. Press play in your DAW and the edits land on the grid.

Can I use it on vocals and drums?

Yes. It was designed around melodic parts, but stutters and tape stops on drum loops and vocal chops work well.

Can I release music made with the presets?

Yes. Everything you make with VELTARA is yours to release, royalty-free.

How many computers can I use?

Up to three at a time, for example a studio Mac, a laptop and a Windows PC. You can move an activation from your account.

How it works

Three moves from plain to finished.

Load it on a melody

Insert VELTARA FX on any instrument or audio track: keys, synths, guitars, arps, vocal chops.

Pick a sound

Browse 150 presets by genre in a visual browser. Each card shows how much texture, movement and space it brings.

Play it by hand

Four macros shape everything: Texture, Movement, Space, Character. Then send the XY pad to one effect and play it like an instrument.

The engine

Ten effect engines. Ten different algorithms.

Glitch editing, granular processing, spectral blur and seven more, each its own processing, not the same effect under another name. Press play on any card to hear that engine alone; hold DRY to compare.

Engine demos: the same arpeggio with only that engine switched on, default macros. The FILTER clip sweeps the cutoff the way you would with the XY pad.

XY pad

A pad you play, not a pad you look at.

  • Pick the effect it plays. Each one has its own two axes: cutoff and resonance for the filter, width and feedback for the delay, size and density for grains.
  • The centre is your preset. Move away to explore, double-click to come back.
  • Smooth and click-free, audible while you drag, and saved with your session.

FAQ

Buying and licenses

Is this a subscription?

Your choice. The Perpetual license is a one-time payment and it is yours. VELTARA Plus is a subscription that adds every monthly expansion pack.

How does rent-to-own work?

After 12 monthly payments of VELTARA Plus (or one yearly payment), your license turns into a perpetual one automatically and the subscription ends by itself: VELTARA FX is yours for good, with the packs from your Plus months, and nothing more is charged. Join Plus again any time for the new packs. If you cancel before that, it keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then switches to demo mode; your projects stay safe and renewing brings everything back.

What is in an expansion pack?

New presets and textures, one pack a month, installed from the PACKS button inside the plugin. Some packs are free for everyone; Plus members get all of them.

How many computers can I use?

Up to three at a time, for example a studio Mac, a laptop and a Windows PC. You can move an activation from your account.

Where do I find my license and downloads?

In your account at store.veltaravst.com: your licenses, activated computers, invoices and installers, all in one place.

Using VELTARA FX

Will the glitch follow my project tempo?

Yes. Slices lock to your DAW's tempo and bar position, from half notes down to 1/32 and triplets. Press play in your DAW and the edits land on the grid.

Can I use it on vocals and drums?

Yes. It was designed around melodic parts, but stutters and tape stops on drum loops and vocal chops work well.

Can I release music made with the presets?

Yes. Everything you make with VELTARA FX is yours to release, royalty-free.

What does demo mode do?

Before you activate (or after a Plus period ends without renewing), VELTARA FX runs as a demo: 8 of the 10 engines (SPECTRAL and HARMONY need a license), the 50 STARTER presets, one instance playing at a time, no saving of your own presets, and 1.5 s of silence once a minute. It never touches your projects: they open and save as usual, and every setting comes back when you activate.

Does it need an internet connection?

To activate (you approve the computer in your browser) and for a quick license check now and then: Perpetual works up to 60 days offline; Plus checks in around each renewal. No internet on the studio computer? Activate it offline with a file. Audio processing never depends on a connection.
